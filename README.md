## Claim
The existing S206 steel columns may not have sufficient load-bearing capacity for the additional loads resulting from the proposed two-storey extension.

The Client Report identifies the S206 columns as 60 × 60 mm solid square steel columns and states that they will likely need to be replaced for the proposed construction.

## Investigation
The structural IFC model will be investigated to identify the S206 steel columns and examine their geometry, dimensions, and material properties.

The available BIM data will be assessed to determine whether sufficient information is available to evaluate the structural capacity of the columns under the proposed additional loading.

## Identified Issues
The Client Report identifies the existing S206 steel columns as potentially inadequate for the increased loads resulting from the proposed two-storey extension.

The capacity of the columns should therefore be verified with updated load calculations, and the columns may need to be strengthened or replaced.

## Possible Solutions
A potential solution is to verify the axial capacity of the S206 steel columns and determine whether they can safely carry the increased loads resulting from the proposed extension.

The existing OpenBIM tool "2520: IFC Column Axial Capacity Analysis" was identified as a possible basis for this investigation. The tool extracts column geometry and material information from an IFC model and uses this information to calculate the axial capacity of columns.

The tool is relevant to the identified issue because it performs a capacity check based on column geometry and material properties. However, the existing tool appears to be developed for concrete columns. It would therefore need to be adapted to use steel material properties before it could be applied to the S206 columns.
